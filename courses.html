Language School

JLPT Japanese — N5 to N1.

Structured preparation for the Japan Foundation's Japanese-Language Proficiency Test, from absolute beginner through advanced professional fluency. Every level is taught by qualified native and near-native instructors.

N5
Entry · 3 months

JLPT N5 — Foundation

Hiragana, katakana and basic kanji. Everyday phrases for travel, shopping and self-introduction.
N4
Elementary · 4 months

JLPT N4 — Elementary

Basic grammar, ~300 kanji, simple conversations and reading of short texts on familiar topics.
N3
Intermediate · 6 months

JLPT N3 — Intermediate

Bridge level. Comprehending newspapers, workplace conversation and structured written communication.
N2
Upper Intermediate · 9 months

JLPT N2 — Career Gateway

Your entry point to a professional career in Japan — required by many employers and SSW programmes. Reading native articles, formal writing.
N1
Advanced · 12 months

JLPT N1 — Professional

The highest JLPT level. Required for medical, engineering and academic professionals working in Japan. Mastery of nuanced, abstract Japanese.
