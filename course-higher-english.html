Programme overview

What you'll learn.

The Higher Diploma in English moves graduates of our Diploma in English — or holders of an equivalent Level 5 qualification — from confident fluency to advanced command of the language. The programme adds the linguistic depth, academic rigour and applied skills needed to teach, translate, write professionally, or progress into a bachelor's degree in English or education.

Learning is seminar-led and practice-heavy: you will analyse texts and discourse, run teaching micro-sessions, complete translation briefs, and produce an extended, individually-supervised writing project to publishable standard.

Key learning outcomes

  • Command advanced academic and professional English across registers and genres
  • Analyse language, discourse and texts using core linguistic frameworks
  • Plan and deliver English lessons using recognized teaching methodology
  • Translate and interpret accurately between English and a local language
  • Write and edit extended works — research, reports and creative pieces
  • Prepare for advanced proficiency benchmarks (IELTS Band 7+ equivalent)

Course curriculum

The programme is structured around 9 advanced modules delivered across two semesters with continuous assessment and an extended project.

01 Advanced Grammar & Usage 4 weeks
Complex structures, stylistic precision, register and the grammar of formal written English.
02 Introduction to Linguistics 5 weeks
Phonetics, morphology, syntax, semantics and how language is structured and used.
03 Academic Writing & Research 5 weeks
Argumentation, referencing, the research essay, and writing for publication and study.
04 Literature & Critical Reading 5 weeks
Close reading of prose, poetry and drama; critical theory and the analytical essay.
05 Teaching English (TESL/TEFL Foundations) 5 weeks
Methodology, lesson planning, classroom management and assessed teaching practice.
06 Translation & Interpreting 4 weeks
Translation theory and practice between English and a local language, with live briefs.
07 Professional & Media Writing 4 weeks
Copywriting, editing, journalism and content writing for digital and print.
08 Advanced Speaking & Discourse 3 weeks
Debate, public speaking, pragmatics and the analysis of spoken discourse.
09 Capstone Extended Project 9 weeks
An individually-supervised research dissertation or major portfolio, defended before a language panel.

Career outcomes

Higher diploma graduates step into teaching, writing and communication roles — and articulate into the final year of partnered bachelor's degrees.

English Teacher / Lecturer

Teach English in schools, institutes and language centres.

TESL / TEFL Instructor

Teach English as a second or foreign language at home and abroad.

Translator / Interpreter

Professional translation and interpreting across sectors.

Editor / Proofreader

Edit, proof and shape written content to publication standard.

Content & Copywriter

Lead writing for brands, publishers and digital media.

Communications Officer

Own PR, internal comms and corporate messaging.

Journalist / Sub-Editor

Report, write and edit for print and online newsrooms.

Examiner / Materials Writer

Develop assessments and learning materials for English programmes.

Bachelor's (Final Year)

Articulate into the final year of a partnered English or education degree.

Eligibility & entry requirements

  • Diploma in English (NVQ Level 5) or an equivalent qualification
  • Alternatively, demonstrated English proficiency (IELTS 6.0+) plus a bridging assessment
  • Open to teachers, writers and university-bound students
  • A short placement assessment to confirm your starting level
  • Successful interview with admissions counsellor
