Programme overview

What you'll learn.

The Diploma in Engineering is a foundational programme that gives students exposure to all three core engineering disciplines — civil, mechanical and electrical — before allowing specialization in the final semester. The structure suits students who want a broad engineering grounding before committing to a specific bachelor's track.

Studio-based learning is central. Students complete drafting, fabrication, and electrical wiring lab work each week, alongside off-campus site visits to construction projects, manufacturing facilities and power infrastructure.

Key learning outcomes

  • Apply engineering mathematics to real structural, mechanical and electrical problems
  • Produce engineering drawings to BS / ISO standards using AutoCAD and SolidWorks
  • Analyze beam loading, material stress, and basic structural elements
  • Wire and test single-phase and three-phase circuits to local safety standards
  • Read and interpret architectural and services drawings on site
  • Plan, schedule and report on a small engineering project

Course curriculum

The programme is structured around 11 core modules delivered across two semesters with continuous assessment and a capstone project.

01 Engineering Mathematics I & II 8 weeks
Calculus, linear algebra, differential equations and applied numerical methods.
02 Engineering Drawing & CAD 6 weeks
Orthographic projection, sectioning, dimensioning. AutoCAD 2D and SolidWorks 3D modelling.
03 Mechanics & Materials 6 weeks
Statics, dynamics, stress and strain, material properties, and basic failure analysis.
04 Electrical Circuits & Machines 6 weeks
DC and AC circuit analysis, transformers, motors. Lab wiring and measurement.
05 Thermodynamics & Fluid Mechanics 5 weeks
Heat transfer, thermodynamic cycles, fluid statics and dynamics with engineering applications.
06 Structural Analysis & Construction 6 weeks
Beam and frame analysis, reinforced concrete basics, soil mechanics introduction.
07 Manufacturing & Workshop Practice 5 weeks
Machining, welding, fabrication and safety. Supervised workshop sessions.
08 Electronics & Microcontrollers 5 weeks
Semiconductor basics, op-amps, Arduino-based prototyping and embedded basics.
09 Engineering Project Management 4 weeks
Scheduling, cost estimation, risk, and contract awareness for engineering projects.
10 Engineering Ethics & Sustainability 3 weeks
Professional conduct, environmental impact, and sustainability standards.
11 Capstone Engineering Project 10 weeks
A team design-build-test project across one of the three core disciplines, defended before industry assessors.

Career outcomes

Graduates progress into roles across local and international employers — and many continue into our partnered international bachelor's degrees.

Civil Site Engineer

Supervise construction works and ensure design compliance on site.

Mechanical Technician

Operate, maintain and troubleshoot mechanical systems and machinery.

Electrical Technician

Install, test and maintain electrical systems across buildings and industry.

CAD / Design Drafter

Produce technical drawings for engineering consultancies and contractors.

Quantity Surveyor (Junior)

Measure, value and report on construction works.

Project Coordinator

Track schedules, budgets and resources on engineering projects.

Maintenance Engineer

Preventive and corrective maintenance across plant and facilities.

Site Supervisor

Coordinate trades and ensure quality and safety on construction sites.

Trainee Building Services Engineer

Support HVAC, electrical and plumbing services design and installation.

Eligibility & entry requirements

  • GCE Advanced Level — 3 passes including Mathematics (Science / Tech stream preferred)
  • GCE O/L credit pass in Mathematics, Science and English
  • Industrial Technology / Bio Systems Tech / Engineering Tech stream candidates welcome
  • Mature applicants considered with relevant technician-level experience
