Quick answers

Before you ask.

A few of the most common questions we receive. For anything else, the admissions team is one phone call away.

Call admissions
Can I apply online?
Yes. Use the form above and an admissions counsellor will be in touch within one working day. You'll receive guidance on documents required and the next steps for your chosen programme.
When are the next intakes?
Diploma programmes run three intakes per year (January, May, September). The JLPT language school has monthly N5 / N4 intakes, with twice-yearly N3 / N2 / N1 cohorts aligned to the JLPT examination schedule.
Do you offer scholarships?
Yes — merit-based scholarships are available for top GCE A/L performers, and need-based bursaries for qualifying students. Ask your admissions counsellor for eligibility criteria.
Can I visit campus before enrolling?
Absolutely. Walk-in tours are welcome during office hours, and we host a structured Open Day each January and July with curriculum demos, classroom visits and counselling sessions.
