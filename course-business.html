Programme overview

What you'll learn.

The Diploma in Business Management is designed for entry-level professionals and aspiring entrepreneurs who want a rigorous, applied grounding in how organizations actually work. The programme blends classic management theory with current practice — Sri Lankan and global case studies, live-firm assignments, and a capstone business project assessed by an industry panel.

Students graduate with the ability to read a financial statement, plan a marketing campaign end-to-end, manage a small team, and present a defensible business case to senior decision-makers.

Key learning outcomes

  • Analyze and interpret financial statements to support management decisions
  • Design and execute a multi-channel marketing campaign with measurable KPIs
  • Apply core HR practices including recruitment, performance management, and labour law compliance
  • Build an operational plan covering supply chain, quality, and customer service
  • Draft and defend a strategic business plan supported by market and competitive analysis
  • Lead a small team through change, conflict, and goal-setting cycles

Course curriculum

The programme is structured around 10 core modules delivered across two semesters with continuous assessment and a capstone project.

01 Principles of Management 4 weeks
Foundations of management thought, organizational structure, planning, organizing, leading and controlling. Includes a comparative study of management styles across regions.
02 Business Communication 3 weeks
Professional writing, presentations, negotiation, and intercultural communication. Practical sessions on client emails, board memos, and pitch decks.
03 Financial Accounting 5 weeks
Accounting equation, double-entry, preparation and interpretation of income statements, balance sheets and cash flow statements. Introduction to LKAS standards.
04 Marketing Fundamentals 4 weeks
Marketing mix, segmentation, brand positioning, digital channels, and campaign measurement. Includes a live mini-campaign for a local SME.
05 Human Resource Management 4 weeks
Recruitment, training, performance appraisal, compensation, and Sri Lankan labour law essentials.
06 Operations & Supply Chain 4 weeks
Process design, capacity planning, inventory management, procurement, and quality systems.
07 Business Statistics & Analytics 4 weeks
Descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, regression, and dashboard reporting using Excel and Power BI.
08 Strategic Management 4 weeks
Industry analysis, generic strategies, blue-ocean thinking, and strategic execution frameworks.
09 Entrepreneurship & New Ventures 3 weeks
Opportunity identification, lean validation, financing, and the legal mechanics of starting a business in Sri Lanka.
10 Capstone Business Project 7 weeks
A full business plan or consulting engagement, defended before an industry panel of marketers, CFOs and entrepreneurs.
