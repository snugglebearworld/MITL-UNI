Welcome to

MITL Campus.

MITL Campus is a premier platform for professional education — focused on helping individuals and businesses across Sri Lanka achieve their highest potential.

We deliver practical, industry-focused training across Business, Marketing, Technology and Leadership. Our offering spans certifications, workshops, online courses, and in-person training — designed to serve every stage of a professional career.

From entry-level professionals starting their journey, to career developers seeking advancement, to leadership-level professionals refining their craft, and organizations seeking workforce upskilling — MITL meets each learner where they are.

What services does MITL Campus offer?
We deliver a full ladder of professional qualifications — certificate programmes, diplomas, HND programmes, bachelor and master pathways with our partner universities. We also offer specialist training in nursing, caregiving, preschool teaching, and an extensive language school across 13 world languages.
Can I complete UGC-recognized qualifications through MITL Campus?
Yes — MITL Campus partners with UGC-recognized universities and is a member of the International Association of Universities (IAU). Graduates of partnered programmes receive official convocations conducted at recognized venues such as the BMICH.
Do you offer online qualification verification?
All MITL certificates are independently verifiable through Certitrust.lk, giving employers worldwide an instant, tamper-proof way to confirm credentials.
What languages do you teach?
We currently offer classes across thirteen languages:
  • Japanese (JLPT N5–N1)
  • Chinese · Korean · Hindi · Tamil
  • German · French · Italian · Danish · Romanian
  • Hebrew · Arabic · English
Can I attend a graduation ceremony?
Yes. Annual convocations are conducted at the BMICH in Colombo. Students completing partner-university qualifications are also eligible to attend the parent institution's graduation ceremony where applicable.
