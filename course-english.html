Programme overview

What you'll learn.

The Diploma in English is built for students and working adults who need real, usable English — not grammar drills for their own sake. Across twelve months you will move from confident everyday conversation to the professional and academic English expected by universities, employers and migration programmes around the world.

Every module is communicative and practical. You'll deliver presentations, sit mock interviews, write the documents you'll actually need — emails, reports, statements of purpose, CVs — and build toward an internationally recognized proficiency benchmark equivalent to IELTS Band 6+.

Key learning outcomes

  • Hold fluent, confident conversations on everyday, academic and workplace topics
  • Write clear professional documents — emails, reports, CVs and statements of purpose
  • Apply advanced grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation accurately and naturally
  • Read and summarize complex texts, articles and instructions with comprehension
  • Deliver structured presentations and perform well in interviews and discussions
  • Prepare for international proficiency tests (IELTS / TOEFL) at Band 6+ level

Course curriculum

The programme is structured around 10 core modules delivered across two semesters with continuous assessment and a capstone project.

01 Foundations of English Grammar 5 weeks
Tenses, sentence structure, parts of speech and common error correction. A clean grammatical base for everything that follows.
02 Spoken English & Pronunciation 4 weeks
Fluency-building conversation, intonation, stress and accent neutralization through daily speaking practice.
03 Listening & Comprehension 3 weeks
Understanding native speech, accents, news, lectures and instructions. Note-taking and gist-versus-detail skills.
04 Reading & Vocabulary 4 weeks
Skimming, scanning and critical reading of articles and texts. Systematic vocabulary expansion and word families.
05 Writing Skills 4 weeks
Paragraphing, essays, summaries and the writing process — planning, drafting, editing and proofreading.
06 Business & Professional English 4 weeks
Emails, reports, meetings, telephone English and workplace etiquette. Includes a mock interview series.
07 Academic English 4 weeks
Academic writing conventions, referencing, statements of purpose, and essay structure for university applications.
08 Presentation & Public Speaking 3 weeks
Structuring talks, visual aids, body language and handling questions. Assessed live presentations.
09 IELTS / TOEFL Exam Preparation 5 weeks
Test format, strategies and full timed practice across all four skills, with individual band feedback.
10 Capstone Communication Project 5 weeks
A portfolio of written work and a final assessed presentation defended before a language panel.

Graduates progress into roles across local and international employers — and many continue into our partnered international bachelor's degrees.

Teach English in schools, institutes and private tuition settings.

Customer Service Executive

Front-line and contact-centre roles for local and international firms.

Content Writer

Write web, marketing and editorial content for brands and agencies.

Administrative Officer

Call Centre Agent

Voice and chat support for global BPO and outsourcing operations.

Translator / Interpreter

Bridge English with local languages in business and services.

Front Office / Hospitality

Guest-facing roles in hotels, airlines and tourism.

Communications Assistant

Support PR, social media and internal communications teams.

University-Bound Student

Meet the English entry requirements for study at home and abroad.

Eligibility & entry requirements

  • GCE Ordinary Level (or equivalent) with a pass in English
  • Open to school leavers, working adults and university-bound students
  • A short placement assessment to confirm your starting level
  • No prior diploma required — beginners through intermediate are welcome
