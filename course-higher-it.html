Programme overview

What you'll learn.

The Higher Diploma in Information Technology takes graduates of our Diploma in IT — or holders of an equivalent Level 5 qualification — from competent practitioner to job-ready software and cloud professional. The year is built around larger, production-realistic systems: services that scale, pipelines that ship, and architectures that hold up under load.

It maps closely to the final year of partnered computing bachelor's degrees, and every module feeds a portfolio. The capstone is a full, deployed, documented system built and operated as a team.

Key learning outcomes

  • Architect and build full-stack applications using modern frameworks and patterns
  • Design, deploy and operate cloud-native services with containers and CI/CD
  • Model, query and optimize data across relational and NoSQL stores
  • Apply secure-by-design principles and assess systems against real threats
  • Build data pipelines and apply analytics and machine-learning fundamentals
  • Work to professional software-engineering practice in an agile team

Course curriculum

The programme is structured around 9 advanced modules delivered across two semesters with continuous assessment and a capstone project.

01 Advanced Software Engineering 5 weeks
Design patterns, clean architecture, testing strategy, and building maintainable systems at scale.
02 Cloud Architecture & DevOps 5 weeks
Microservices, containers and orchestration, infrastructure-as-code, and CI/CD on AWS or Azure.
03 Advanced Databases & Data Modelling 4 weeks
Query optimization, transactions, NoSQL, and data warehousing for analytical workloads.
04 Cybersecurity & Secure Development 4 weeks
Threat modelling, secure coding, identity and access, and a hands-on penetration-testing lab.
05 Data Analytics & Machine Learning 5 weeks
Data pipelines, exploratory analysis, and supervised and unsupervised ML fundamentals in Python.
06 Mobile & Modern Front-End 4 weeks
Component-driven front-ends with React, plus cross-platform mobile fundamentals.
07 Networks & Distributed Systems 4 weeks
Scalability, caching, messaging, fault tolerance and the realities of distributed design.
08 Research & Professional Practice 3 weeks
Technical research methods, documentation, ethics, and agile team practice for the capstone.
09 Capstone Systems Project 9 weeks
A team-built, deployed and documented production system, defended before an industry panel.

Career outcomes

Higher diploma graduates step into developer and engineering roles — and articulate into the final year of partnered bachelor's degrees.

Software Engineer

Design and build production applications across the stack.

Cloud / DevOps Engineer

Operate cloud platforms, pipelines and infrastructure-as-code.

Full-Stack Developer

Own features end-to-end from front-end to database.

Data Analyst / Engineer

Build pipelines and turn data into decisions and dashboards.

Cybersecurity Analyst

Assess, monitor and harden systems against real threats.

Systems / Solutions Engineer

Design and integrate scalable, reliable technical solutions.

Mobile Developer

Build cross-platform mobile applications for clients.

QA Automation Engineer

Design automated testing and quality pipelines.

Bachelor's (Final Year)

Articulate into the final year of a partnered international degree.

Eligibility & entry requirements

  • Diploma in Information Technology (NVQ Level 5) or an equivalent qualification
  • Alternatively, a related Level 5 diploma plus a successful bridging assessment
  • Demonstrated programming ability (portfolio or short technical assessment)
  • Mature applicants with relevant IT experience considered
  • Successful interview with admissions counsellor
