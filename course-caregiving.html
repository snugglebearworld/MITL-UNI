Programme overview

Your pathway to Israel.

The State of Israel operates a structured home-caregiver visa programme that places qualified foreign caregivers with elderly and disabled Israeli citizens. Sri Lankan women represent one of the most sought-after nationalities in this programme — and MITL's six-month training ensures you arrive fully prepared, legally compliant, and professionally confident.

This programme covers everything from clinical caregiving techniques and medication safety to conversational Hebrew, Israeli labor rights, and pre-departure documentation. Graduates hold a TVEC-recognized qualification and receive placement support through MITL's licensed recruitment partners.

Why Israel? Israel's National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi) subsidizes home caregiving for elderly citizens, creating a stable, government-backed demand for qualified caregivers. Remuneration ranges from USD 1,000–1,400 per month with accommodation and meals typically provided. Contracts are renewable and many caregivers serve multiple rotations.

Key learning outcomes

  • Safely perform personal care tasks including bathing, dressing, feeding, and mobility assistance
  • Monitor vital signs and recognize early warning signs requiring medical escalation
  • Administer prescribed medications accurately and maintain care documentation
  • Apply gerontology principles and dementia-sensitive communication techniques
  • Communicate in basic Hebrew for daily caregiving situations, instruction-following, and emergencies
  • Understand Israeli labor law, your rights and obligations as a visa holder, and complaint mechanisms
  • Manage cultural differences and build respectful, trusted relationships with care recipients and families
  • Respond to emergencies including falls, choking, cardiac events, and fire — and operate a defibrillator
  • Prepare and maintain nutritional meals aligned with dietary restrictions (kosher, diabetic, dysphagia)

Course curriculum

The programme is structured across 11 modules delivered over six months, combining classroom instruction, skills labs, and a supervised clinical placement at a local care facility.

01 Foundations of Caregiving 2 weeks
Introduction to professional caregiving — roles, responsibilities, ethics, infection control, and the rights and dignity of care recipients. Includes personal protective equipment (PPE) usage and universal precautions.
02 Anatomy, Physiology & Health Basics 2 weeks
Fundamental human body systems relevant to caregiving — musculoskeletal, cardiovascular, respiratory, neurological and digestive. Understanding what is normal and what requires concern.
03 Elderly Care & Gerontology 3 weeks
Physical and psychological changes of aging, age-related conditions (arthritis, osteoporosis, vision/hearing loss), dementia and Alzheimer's care, and person-centered communication strategies.
04 Personal Care & Daily Living Assistance 3 weeks
Practical skills: bathing and hygiene, oral care, dressing, repositioning and safe transfer techniques, continence care, bed-making, and supporting independent daily activities. Hands-on lab sessions.
05 Nutrition & Meal Preparation 2 weeks
Balanced nutrition for elderly care recipients, kosher dietary laws and food handling, diabetic meal planning, preparation of soft and purée diets for dysphagia patients, hydration monitoring.
06 Medication Assistance & Vital Signs 3 weeks
Reading and following prescriptions, safe medication assistance (dispensing pre-prepared doses), measuring and recording blood pressure, pulse, temperature, oxygen saturation, and blood glucose. Recognizing adverse reactions and when to escalate.
07 First Aid & Emergency Response 2 weeks
CPR and AED operation, choking response (Heimlich manoeuvre), fall management, wound care, burn treatment, stroke recognition (FAST), and emergency communication in Hebrew and English. Certified first aid examination included.
08 Hebrew Language for Caregivers 4 weeks
Conversational Hebrew focused on caregiving vocabulary — greetings, instructions, body parts, pain descriptions, food and medication terminology, shopping, and emergency phrases. Emphasis on listening comprehension and clear spoken communication.
09 Israeli Culture, Society & Labor Law 2 weeks
Jewish traditions, religious observances (Shabbat, holidays), cultural etiquette, and household norms. Israeli labor law covering caregiver visa conditions, working hours, rest entitlements, salary rights, contract terms, complaint channels, and SRILKA / SLBFE regulations.
10 Care for Disability & Chronic Conditions 2 weeks
Supporting care recipients with stroke, Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis, diabetes and post-surgical recovery. Assistive device usage (wheelchairs, walkers, hoists), pain management awareness, and palliative care principles.
11 Supervised Clinical Placement 5 weeks
Full-time supervised practical placement at a partnered elderly care home or hospital-adjacent care unit in Sri Lanka. Competency-based assessment across all core care domains. Graded log-book submission required for programme completion.

Career outcomes

Graduates are placed as licensed home caregivers in Israel, with pathways into broader international healthcare employment.

Home Caregiver – Israel

Live-in or live-out caregiver for elderly Israeli citizens under the Bituach Leumi scheme.

Live-in Personal Carer

Full-time residential caregiver providing 24-hour support and companionship to a single recipient.

Elderly Care Support Worker

Assist residents in nursing homes or assisted-living facilities in Israel and beyond.

Hospital Care Aide

Support registered nurses in hospital wards, rehabilitation centres, and day-care units.

Disability Support Worker

Provide daily living assistance to adults with physical or intellectual disabilities.

Palliative Care Assistant

Offer compassionate end-of-life care and family support within hospice environments.

Eligibility & entry requirements

  • Female applicants aged 23–45 (in line with Israeli caregiver visa guidelines)
  • GCE O/L qualification or equivalent (minimum 5 subjects including English and Sinhala/Tamil)
  • Physically fit and in good health — medical fitness examination required prior to enrolment
  • No serious criminal record — police clearance certificate required
  • Genuine willingness to work abroad; family consent letter recommended
  • Basic English literacy for instructional comprehension and documentation
  • Candidates with prior caregiving, nursing aid, or domestic service experience are encouraged to apply

Certification & recognition

  • MITL Diploma in Caregiving — verifiable at Certitrust.lk
  • TVEC (Tertiary & Vocational Education Commission) recognized qualification — NVQ Level 3
  • SLBFE (Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment) compliant pre-departure training
  • First Aid & CPR Certificate (internationally recognized)
  • Accepted by MITL's licensed Israeli placement agency partners
