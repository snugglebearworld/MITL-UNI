Programme overview

What you'll learn.

The Higher Diploma in Business Management is the natural next step for graduates of our Diploma in Business Management — or holders of an equivalent Level 5 qualification — who want to move from operational competence to managerial judgement. The programme deepens every core discipline and adds the strategic, financial and research capability expected of an emerging manager.

It is also the closest articulation point into a partnered international bachelor's degree, with several modules mapped directly to final-year undergraduate study. A substantial research-led management project sits at the centre of the year.

Key learning outcomes

  • Formulate and evaluate corporate strategy in competitive and uncertain markets
  • Interpret advanced financial statements and build management accounting models
  • Lead teams and manage organizational change at a departmental level
  • Design and conduct an applied business research project end-to-end
  • Apply data analytics to support evidence-based management decisions
  • Operate confidently across governance, risk and corporate responsibility

Course curriculum

The programme is structured around 9 advanced modules delivered across two semesters with continuous assessment and a research project.

01 Strategic Management 5 weeks
Corporate and competitive strategy, industry analysis, strategic options and execution at scale.
02 Corporate Finance & Management Accounting 5 weeks
Capital budgeting, cost of capital, budgeting and variance analysis for decision-making.
03 Leadership & Organizational Behaviour 4 weeks
Leadership models, motivation, team dynamics, and managing organizational culture.
04 Marketing Management 4 weeks
Strategic marketing, brand management, customer analytics and integrated campaign planning.
05 Operations & Project Management 4 weeks
Process improvement, lean and quality systems, and managing projects to time, cost and scope.
06 Business Analytics & Decision Making 4 weeks
Data-driven decision frameworks, forecasting, and dashboarding with Excel and Power BI.
07 Corporate Governance, Risk & Ethics 3 weeks
Governance structures, enterprise risk, compliance and corporate social responsibility.
08 Research Methods for Business 4 weeks
Research design, qualitative and quantitative methods, and academic writing for the project.
09 Capstone Management Research Project 9 weeks
An individual research-led business project, supervised and defended before an academic and industry panel.

Career outcomes

Higher diploma graduates step into supervisory and junior-management roles — and articulate into the final year of partnered bachelor's degrees.

Assistant Manager

Support departmental leadership across operations, sales or services.

Business Development Executive

Identify, pitch and close growth opportunities and partnerships.

Management Accountant (Junior)

Budgeting, costing and management reporting to support decisions.

Marketing Manager (Junior)

Own brand and campaign strategy with measurable outcomes.

Operations Executive

Drive process improvement and coordinate cross-functional delivery.

HR Executive

Lead recruitment, performance and people development initiatives.

Project Officer

Plan, run and report on organizational projects and change.

Business Analyst

Analyse data and processes to inform strategy and investment.

Bachelor's (Final Year)

Articulate into the final year of a partnered international degree.

Eligibility & entry requirements

  • Diploma in Business Management (NVQ Level 5) or an equivalent qualification
  • Alternatively, a related Level 5 diploma plus a successful bridging assessment
  • Mature applicants with substantial supervisory experience considered
  • Working proficiency in English (written and spoken)
  • Successful interview with admissions counsellor
