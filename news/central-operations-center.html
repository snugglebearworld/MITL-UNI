MITL opens Central Operations Center in Boralesgamuwa.

Date   18 January 2026 Venue   Central Operations Center, Boralesgamuwa Author   MITL Communications Read   5 min

MITL Campus has formally launched its new Central Operations Center in Boralesgamuwa, marking a major milestone in the institution's long-running modernisation programme. Co-located with the campus's principal academic block at Raththanapitiya, the new facility will consolidate the operational backbone of MITL into a single, dedicated hub.

The opening was marked by a traditional Sri Lankan lamp-lighting ceremony attended by senior leadership, faculty, partner-institution representatives and members of the operations and student-services teams who will work from the new premises day-to-day.

What the centre will do

The Central Operations Center is designed as the nerve centre for the campus's nationwide footprint. Four core functions are now consolidated under one roof:

Campus operations. Day-to-day coordination across MITL's academic blocks — timetabling, faculty scheduling, examinations logistics, and facilities management — is now run from a single operations desk, replacing the previous split across two separate offices.

Student data & records. Admissions, enrolment, transcripts and certification records for current students and alumni are managed centrally, with a secure on-premises records room supporting the institution's data-protection commitments.

Online learning platforms. A dedicated technology team oversees the campus's digital learning environment, hosted virtual classrooms, lecture-capture systems and the student portal — ensuring continuity for the growing cohort of part-time and distance learners.

Educational affiliations. The centre also houses the international office, which manages relationships with The Universidad Azteca, Universitas BTH, the Japan Foundation and other affiliated institutions, alongside accreditation bodies including UGC, IAU, CPD, UNESCO and Certitrust.

A campus of this size cannot run out of a back office any more. Bringing operations, student records, online learning and our partnership work into a single dedicated centre is the foundation we need to keep growing without losing the personal touch our students rely on. — Mr. Milinda N. Lamahewa, Chairman

What students will notice

For current students and alumni, the most immediate change is a single point of contact for all administrative requests — from transcript reissues and certification verification to enrolment changes and articulation paperwork. The centre operates extended hours and supports walk-in, telephone and online channels.

For prospective students and partner institutions, the centre now hosts a dedicated meeting space for in-person consultations, MoU discussions, and visiting delegations from MITL's international partners.

Next steps

The Central Operations Center will run alongside the existing academic offices during a six-week transition window, with full cut-over to the new premises completing by the end of March. Operating hours, contact channels and a virtual tour of the new facility are being published on the MITL website and circulated to the alumni network.
