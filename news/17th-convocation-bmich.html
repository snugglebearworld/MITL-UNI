17th Annual Convocation honours 612 graduates at BMICH.

Date   14 May 2026 Venue   BMICH, Colombo Author   MITL Communications Read   8 min

The Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) hosted the seventeenth annual convocation of MITL Campus on Saturday morning, in a ceremony that conferred diplomas and partner-university degrees on 612 graduates drawn from the Boralesgamuwa campus and its associated language and professional schools.

Graduates processed in regalia across the main hall to receive their certifications from Chief Guest Prof. Asanga Tilakaratne, Senior Academic and Strategic Advisor, alongside Chairman Mr. Milinda N. Lamahewa and visiting representatives of The Universidad Azteca and Universitas BTH — two of MITL's principal international academic partners.

A record graduating cohort

The 2026 ceremony marked the largest cohort in the campus's seventeen-year history. Of the 612 graduates, 274 received the Diploma in Business Management, 198 the Diploma in Information Technology, 84 the Diploma in Engineering, and 56 graduates received JLPT-aligned certifications across N3 through N1.

A further 38 students received their bachelor's and master's degrees jointly awarded with The Universidad Azteca and Universitas BTH — graduates of the articulation pathways MITL established with these institutions over the past four years.

A turning point is rarely a single moment — it is the patient accumulation of weeks of effort that suddenly reveals a new direction. What you celebrate today is not a finish line, but the moment you can finally see the road clearly. — Prof. Asanga Tilakaratne, Chief Guest

Awards & honours

The Chairman's Gold Medal for academic excellence was awarded to Ms. Thisuli Karunaratne of the Diploma in Information Technology cohort, who maintained a perfect grade-point average across all twelve modules of her programme.

Additional honours included the Industry Impact Award presented to a final-year business project on rural-microfinance digitization, and the JLPT Excellence Medal awarded to two students who passed the N1 examination on their first attempt — a rare distinction worldwide.

International pathways

Twelve members of the graduating cohort have already confirmed enrolment at The Universidad Azteca in Mexico for the September intake, continuing into bachelor's and master's programmes in business administration and information systems. A further nine will progress to Universitas BTH in Indonesia for the engineering pathway.

The convocation concluded with a closing address by the Chairman, who reaffirmed the campus's commitment to expanding its language school, broadening engineering specializations, and deepening employer partnerships in Sri Lanka and abroad.
