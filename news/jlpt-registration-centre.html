MITL becomes designated JLPT registration centre.

Date   07 December 2025 Venue   MITL Examination Hall Author   MITL Language School Read   4 min

The Japan Foundation has confirmed MITL Campus as a designated registration and testing centre for the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT), with the first sitting hosted on campus on the first Sunday of December.

Just over two hundred candidates registered for the December examination across N5, N4, N3 and N2 levels — a mix of MITL's own diploma students and external candidates from the Colombo and Kalutara districts who chose Boralesgamuwa as their nearest accredited testing site.

What the designation means

JLPT testing centres operate under strict procedures set by the Japan Educational Exchanges and Services foundation, which administers the examination worldwide. Designated centres must meet requirements covering hall acoustics, candidate seating, listening-audio playback, invigilator training, and chain-of-custody procedures for examination materials.

MITL's newly refurbished examination hall on the second floor of the Boralesgamuwa campus completed the inspection process in October. The facility seats up to 240 candidates simultaneously and is equipped with a dedicated audio system for the listening component.

Being able to sit JLPT on the campus where you study removes a real barrier — particularly for our N2 and N3 candidates who balance preparation with work or other studies. We are proud to host this examination for our students and for the wider community. — Ms. Hiruni Senanayake, Head of the Language School

Looking ahead

The next JLPT sitting will be the July 2026 examination, with registration opening in March via the Japan Foundation portal. MITL students preparing for N5 through N1 receive dedicated registration support and mock examinations from the language school's faculty in the eight weeks leading up to each sitting.
