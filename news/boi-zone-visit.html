Business diploma cohort visits BOI investment zone.

Date   11 November 2025 Venue   Katunayake EPZ Author   School of Business Read   4 min

The full second-year cohort of the Diploma in Business Management spent the day at the Katunayake Export Processing Zone in early November, in a structured industrial tour arranged in partnership with the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka (BOI).

The visit took 142 students through three contrasting manufacturing operations: an apparel exporter operating to European retail customers, a precision-electronics assembly line, and a food-processing facility producing for the regional South Asian market.

What students saw

At each site, plant managers walked the cohort through end-to-end operations — from raw-material intake and inventory control through assembly, quality assurance, shipping and post-shipment customer support. Briefings covered topics from the textbook (lean manufacturing, ISO compliance, just-in-time logistics) and dimensions students rarely encounter in lectures, including BOI tax incentive structures, multi-country supply-chain risk, and the labour-economics realities of zone-based employment.

You can teach Porter's value chain on a whiteboard, but the moment a student walks the floor of a 400-person assembly line they understand the constraints in a way no textbook conveys. The BOI partnership lets us do that. — Dr. Nilanthi Wickramasinghe, Head of Business School

Why it matters

The visit is the first of three industrial tours embedded in the second-year diploma curriculum. The next tour, scheduled for February, will focus on financial-services operations in Colombo's central business district.
