Programme overview

What you'll learn.

The Higher Diploma in Engineering builds on the broad foundation of our Diploma in Engineering — or an equivalent Level 5 qualification — and lets students specialize in civil, mechanical or electrical engineering. The programme deepens analytical capability, introduces professional design codes, and is delivered through design studios, instrumented labs and extended site work.

It is the closest articulation point into the final years of partnered engineering bachelor's degrees, and culminates in a substantial, individually-assessed design-build-test project within your chosen discipline.

Key learning outcomes

  • Apply advanced engineering analysis within a chosen specialist discipline
  • Design to recognized national and international engineering codes and standards
  • Use industry CAD, simulation and analysis tools to a professional standard
  • Plan, cost and manage an engineering project through its full lifecycle
  • Conduct instrumented testing and interpret results against design intent
  • Communicate engineering decisions through reports, drawings and defences

Course curriculum

The programme is structured around 9 advanced modules including a specialization stream, delivered across three semesters with a major project.

01 Advanced Engineering Mathematics 6 weeks
Differential equations, transforms, numerical methods and applied statistics for engineering analysis.
02 Engineering Analysis & Simulation 6 weeks
Finite-element thinking, modelling and simulation using industry software across disciplines.
03 Specialization: Civil Engineering 8 weeks
Structural design, reinforced concrete and steel, geotechnics, and construction management.
04 Specialization: Mechanical Engineering 8 weeks
Machine design, advanced thermodynamics and fluids, manufacturing systems and maintenance.
05 Specialization: Electrical Engineering 8 weeks
Power systems, control, electrical machines, and building services design and protection.
06 Engineering Design Studio 6 weeks
Design to codes and standards, tolerance and safety, and integrated multidisciplinary design.
07 Project & Engineering Management 4 weeks
Planning, estimation, procurement, contracts and quality assurance for engineering works.
08 Research Methods & Professional Ethics 4 weeks
Technical research, sustainability, professional conduct and engineering report writing.
09 Capstone Design-Build-Test Project 12 weeks
An individual specialist project from concept through design, build and test, defended before industry assessors.

Career outcomes

Higher diploma graduates step into technician-engineer and supervisory roles — and articulate into the final years of partnered bachelor's degrees.

Civil / Structural Technician-Engineer

Support design and supervise construction to specification.

Mechanical Engineer (Junior)

Design, test and maintain mechanical systems and plant.

Electrical Engineer (Junior)

Design and commission electrical and building-services systems.

Design / CAD Engineer

Produce and analyse designs for consultancies and contractors.

Site / Project Engineer

Run engineering works to time, cost, quality and safety.

Quantity Surveyor

Measure, value and control cost across construction projects.

Maintenance / Reliability Engineer

Plan and lead preventive and corrective maintenance programmes.

Building Services Engineer

Design HVAC, electrical and plumbing services for buildings.

Bachelor's (Final Years)

Articulate into the later years of a partnered engineering degree.

Eligibility & entry requirements

  • Diploma in Engineering (NVQ Level 5) or an equivalent qualification
  • Alternatively, a related Level 5 technical diploma plus a bridging assessment
  • Sound grounding in engineering mathematics
  • Mature applicants with relevant technician-level experience considered
  • Successful interview with admissions counsellor
